## 🎬 What's Up? - Philosophical Cinema Analysis

**What's Up?** is an autonomous philosophical media engine — an AI-powered platform that explores the deeper meanings hidden within cinema and television. We go beyond reviews to uncover the existential, metaphysical, and philosophical themes that make films profound.

### Our Philosophy

We believe every film and TV series contains layers of meaning waiting to be uncovered. Beyond plot summaries and star ratings, there exist:

- **Existential questions** about life, death, and purpose
- **Metaphysical themes** exploring reality, consciousness, and time
- **Human conditions** that mirror our own struggles and triumphs
- **Philosophical frameworks** that help us understand our world

### How It Works

This site is entirely automated, generating 4-5 fresh analyses every day:

| Day | Posts |
|-----|-------|
| Monday - Saturday | 4 posts (2 movies + 2 series) |
| Sunday | 5 posts (special edition) |

Each post is crafted by AI that has been trained to think like a film philosopher — not a critic, but an explorer of meaning.

### The Content

Every analysis includes:

- 🖼️ **High-quality imagery** from TMDB
- 🧠 **Philosophical deep-dive** exploring themes and meanings
- 📺 **Streaming availability** where to watch
- 🏷️ **Mood tags** for emotional categorization
- 💬 **Community discussion** via Giscus comments

### Curated Selection

Our film and series selection comes from carefully curated IMDb lists covering:

- Classic Hollywood masterpieces
- International cinema gems
- Award-winning TV series
- Hidden cult favorites
- Modern blockbusters with depth
